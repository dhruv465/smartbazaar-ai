Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers Instinct for SmartBazaar (Meta app name: SmartbazarAI), an experimental WhatsApp AI shopping assistant operated by Dhruv Sathe. It explains how the prototype handles information you share with it.

This is an independent demonstration, not an official service of Reliance Retail, SmartBazaar or JioMart. Confirmed orders use JioCommerce Platform's development order system and its test inventory; historical mock orders remain simulated. No physical purchases, payments or deliveries are processed.

Information we receive

  • WhatsApp messages: your phone number, available profile name, text, button selections, message timestamps, and message or media identifiers supplied by WhatsApp.
  • Media you send: voice notes and photos, such as shopping lists or product pictures, for transcription or interpretation.
  • Shopping details: items, quantities, preferences, language, carts and development or mock order history. If you share an address or location, including coordinates, we use it for the demo checkout and may remember it for later conversations.
  • Operational records: replies, processing status, product search results, timing and error information needed to run and troubleshoot the assistant.

How we use information

We use this information to understand your list, find products, prepare and edit your cart, remember your preferences, prepare checkout, submit confirmed development orders, and reply on WhatsApp. Historical mock orders remain simulated. We also use records to prevent duplicate processing, investigate failures and handle support or privacy requests.

When optional voice replies are enabled, the assistant can accompany a written reply with AI-generated speech. The first successfully sent audio reply in a chat identifies itself as AI-generated; later audio replies do not repeat that notice. The voice is AI-generated, not a live person speaking.

AI can misunderstand messages, speech or images. Please check the cart and details before confirming a demo order. You do not need to send payment credentials or identity documents to try the prototype.

Services that process information

  • WhatsApp / Meta carries your messages and our replies, including generated audio uploaded for delivery when voice replies are enabled.
  • OpenAI receives message text, voice audio or transcripts, images, and relevant shopping context to understand your requests. When voice replies are enabled, it also receives the reply text and selected voice settings to generate speech.
  • Render runs the application; Supabase stores its conversation, customer, cart, development or mock order and operational records.
  • JioMart receives product search terms and the prototype's configured demo pincode to return catalog listings.
  • JioCommerce Platform (JCP) receives your cart, name, phone number and delivery address when preparing development checkout. After you tap Place order, it receives the confirmed order and returns development order, shipment and status records. These are test orders, not physical fulfilment.

Dhruv Sathe may access stored records for support, troubleshooting, security and privacy requests. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. These providers may process data outside your country and have their own retention and privacy practices.

Provider information: WhatsApp privacy policy, OpenAI API data controls, Render privacy policy, Supabase privacy policy and JioMart privacy policy.

What stays after a conversation

The application processes downloaded voice audio and images temporarily. It does not archive those media files or save new raw voice transcripts in its conversation database. Resolved shopping details, replies, and the original WhatsApp message metadata can still remain.

Generated audio bytes are also temporary in the application. To prepare and deliver voice replies reliably, its queue stores the reply-derived speech text, selected voice profile, preparation status and WhatsApp media identifier. This does not create an application archive of the audio, and provider copies remain subject to the providers' own practices.

Ordinary text messages, profile and delivery details, carts, development or mock orders, replies and operational records are retained so the conversation can continue. The prototype currently has no automatic deletion schedule for these records. The chat command below clears local profile and conversation records while retaining minimal duplicate-prevention and acceptance records. Orders and customer records already sent to JCP remain there. Deleting a WhatsApp chat on your phone does not delete the application's stored records.

Our handling of temporary media does not mean that WhatsApp, OpenAI or infrastructure providers retain no data. Their policies and account settings apply to their own processing.

Request access, correction or deletion

You can stop using the assistant at any time.

Delete in the chat: send delete my data to the assistant on WhatsApp, then tap Yes, delete (or Haan, mitao). This clears the local saved profile, delivery address and location, cart, order history, completed earlier conversation records and queued replies. Minimal order-acceptance and duplicate-prevention records remain without the saved profile or delivery details. We also retain the deletion request, your phone number and confirmation reply to record and answer the request. This does not cancel or delete orders or customer records already sent to JCP. Later messages are handled as new conversations.

By email: to request a copy or correction of your information, or to ask us to delete it:

  1. Email homescience506@gmail.com with the subject Instinct for SmartBazaar data request.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used, with country code, and whether you want access, correction or deletion. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
  3. We will review the request and may ask you to confirm ownership of the number. For a verified deletion request, we will review linked customer, conversation, cart, order and message records under our control, remove eligible records, and confirm the result by email. We will explain any records we cannot remove and why, including records held by JCP.

Email requests are handled manually. Neither route deletes your WhatsApp account, the chat on your phone, or copies independently held by service providers such as WhatsApp, OpenAI or our hosting providers. If you message the assistant again after deletion, it will create new records.
